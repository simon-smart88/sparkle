Sparkle

A field of randomly coloured squares, redrawn one noisy patch at a time. Inspired by a Tim Head piece, built by Paul Harter. Every channel — red, green, blue, grid resolution, how many pixels change each frame, and how transparent they are — drifts on its own wave. Save a setup you like to the list on the right.
